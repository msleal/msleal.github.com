Capability Model

business outcomes through AI

A working model for taking AI from intent to production: the capabilities a business needs, the layers that deliver them, and the constraints that bound them all. Not a generic maturity chart, and not finished. Like everything on this site it is a work in progress, a map I keep navigating and reshaping as the field moves and as new implementation patterns appear, readable from the business outcome at the top down to the platform that runs it.
